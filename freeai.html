qalabyada AI ee bilaashka kuwa oogu fiican si lacag looga sameeyo

By TechSomali • march 2026 • 5 min read

Free AI Tools to Earn Money in 2026

Hordhac

Sanadka 2026, fursadaha lacag ka sameynta internet-ka ayaa sii kordhaya, waxaana ka mid ah hababka ugu fudud ee loo bilaabi karo isticmaalka qalabka AI ee bilaashka ah. Qalabkan casriga ah waxay kaa caawinayaan qoraalka content, design, videos, iyo automation-ka shaqooyinka online. Maqaalkan wuxuu ku bari doonaa tools-ka bilaashka ah ee ugu fiican, sida loo isticmaalo, casharro iyo strategies gaar ah oo kaa caawinaya inaad lacag ka sameyso online, iyo sidoo kale talooyin SEO-ready si content-kaaga uu u soo jiito traffic badan oo organic ah.

Qalabka AI ee Bilaashka ah ee Lacag Lagu Sameeyo

1. ChatGPT

ChatGPT waa AI awood badan oo kaa caawinaya qoraalka content, blog posts, email marketing, iyo adeegyada chatbot automation. Bilowga, waxaad isticmaali kartaa si aad u qorto articles, scripts, ama emails marketing. Waxa kale oo aad ka sameyn kartaa freelance writing ama copywriting adigoo adeegsanaya platform-yada sida Upwork, Fiverr, ama Freelancer.

Tusaale isticmaal: Qor maqaal 1000–1500 words oo SEO-friendly, ka dibna u isticmaal affiliate marketing ama freelance project.

Booqo ChatGPT

2. Canva AI

Canva AI wuxuu kuu ogolaanayaa inaad si fudud u sameyso designs, graphics, videos, iyo social media content. Templates diyaar ah ayaa kaa caawinaya inaad bilowdo xitaa haddii aadan aqoon design badan u lahayn. Waxaad lacag uga sameyn kartaa freelance designs, YouTube thumbnails, Instagram posts, ama logos.

Tusaale: Samee 10 Instagram posts toddobaadkii freelance ahaan.

Booqo Canva AI

3. Jasper AI

Jasper AI waa content creation automation tool. Waxaad ku qori kartaa blog posts, marketing copy, ama scripts YouTube waqti kooban. Jasper AI waxay taageertaa SEO optimization, taasoo ka dhigaysa content-kaaga mid ka muuqda Google search.

Tusaale: Samee maqaal 2000–3000 words oo SEO ready, ku dar internal links iyo FAQ section.

Booqo Jasper AI

4. Pictory AI

Pictory AI waa video creation tool automated ah. Waxaad isticmaali kartaa si aad u sameyso tutorial videos, marketing videos, ama vlogs. Ku dar videos-ka blog-kaaga ama YouTube channel si aad u kordhiso engagement iyo traffic.

Talo: Ku samee 2–3 videos toddobaadkii bilowga, ka dibna freelance ama monetize YouTube channel-kaaga.

Booqo Pictory AI

5. Copy.ai

Copy.ai waa qalab qoraal automatic ah oo ku habboon marketing content creation. Waxaad ka faa’iidaysan kartaa blog posts, email marketing, iyo social posts. Templates-ka SEO-friendly ayaa kaa caawinaya inaad si fudud u qorto content tayo leh oo traffic keena.

Tusaale: Qor email marketing sequence ama social media captions, ka dibna monetization samee adigoo adeegsanaya affiliate links.

Booqo Copy.ai

Sida Loo Isticmaalo AI Tools-ka Bilaashka ah

  1. Account samee: Dhammaan tools-kan waxay leeyihiin version free ama trial.
  2. Fahm dashboard-ka: Baro menus-ka, templates, iyo functions-ka ugu muhiimsan.
  3. Mashruuc tijaabo bilow: Samee project yar si aad u barato tool-ka.
  4. Monetize content-kaaga: Freelance platforms, blog, YouTube, ama affiliate marketing.
  5. SEO optimize: Isticmaal keywords traffic keeni kara, headings H2/H3, meta description, internal links.
  6. La soco performance: Google Analytics iyo Search Console waxay ku tusayaan traffic-ka iyo ranking-ka content-kaaga.

Strategies Bilowga

  • Diirada saar 2–3 tools si qoto dheer.
  • Balaari knowledge-kaaga si tartiib tartiib ah, isku day tools kale.
  • Freelance writing oo adeegsata ChatGPT iyo Jasper.
  • Designs iyo social media content oo adeegsata Canva AI.
  • Video marketing iyo tutorials oo adeegsata Pictory AI.
  • Affiliate marketing oo adeegsata content ka abuurto Copy.ai.
  • SEO optimized blogging si traffic organic loo helo.

FAQ

Sidee lacag looga sameeyaa ChatGPT?

Freelance writing, content creation, chatbot services, ama digital marketing projects.

Ma lacag baa ku baxaysaa AI tools-kan?

Qaar waa free, qaar subscription. Faham version free vs paid ka hor intaadan bilaabin project-kaaga.

Immisa bilow ah ayaa ku filan?

Ku xirantahay waqtiga iyo skills-kaaga, laakiin bilowga waa inaad tijaabiso 1–2 tool oo online tutorials ka baro.

Sidee blog post u SEO optimize gareeyaa?

Keyword research, headings H2/H3, meta description, internal linking, FAQ section.

Sidee videos uga faa'iidaysto traffic?

Ku dar videos-ka blog post-ka, YouTube, ama social media, link garee content-kaaga si engagement kor loogu qaado.
