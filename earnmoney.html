How to Make $100/Day Using AI (Step-by-Step Guide 2026)

Haddii aad rabto inaad si dhab ah lacag uga sameyso internet-ka, AI (Artificial Intelligence) ayaa maanta noqotay fursad aad u weyn. Dad badan ayaa durba ka sameeya $50 ilaa $200 maalintii iyagoo isticmaalaya tools sida ChatGPT, Fiverr, iyo platforms kale.

Qoraalkan waxaan ku siin doonaa nidaam cad oo aad ku gaari karto $100 maalintii adigoo aan u baahnayn khibrad hore. Waxaad u baahan tahay oo keliya internet, waqti, iyo rabitaan inaad wax barato.

---

Maxaa Ka Dhigay AI Fursad Dahabi ah 2026?

AI waxay badashay sida shaqooyinka online loo sameeyo. Halkii aad saacado badan ku qaadan lahayd shaqo, AI ayaa kaa caawinaysa inaad ku dhameyso daqiiqado gudahood.

  • Automation shaqo badan
  • Waqti badbaadin
  • Productivity sare
  • Fursado lacag oo cusub
---

Step 1: Dooro Skill Lacag Lagu Sameyn Karo

Si aad $100/maalin u gaarto, waa inaad doorataa skill suuqa ka shaqeeya.

Skills-ka Ugu Fiican:

  • Article Writing
  • Copywriting
  • Logo Design
  • Social Media Content
  • Video Script Writing

💡 Talo: Door skill hal ah oo aad ku fiicnaato, kadib ku ballaari.

---

Step 2: Isticmaal AI Tools Sax ah

AI tools ayaa ah sirta guusha. Kuwani waa kuwa ugu fiican:

  • ChatGPT – Qorista content
  • Canva – Design
  • Midjourney – Images
  • CapCut – Video editing

Adigoo isticmaalaya tools-kan, waxaad shaqo ku sameyn kartaa 5x ka dhakhso badan qof caadi ah.

---

Step 3: Samee Fiverr Account

Fiverr waa meesha ugu fudud ee aad ku heli karto clients.

  1. Samee profile professional ah
  2. Ku dar description SEO leh
  3. Samee gig leh keyword sax ah
---

Step 4: Samee Gig Iibin Kara

Gig-gaaga waa inuu noqdaa mid soo jiidasho leh.

Example Gig Title:

“I will write SEO blog posts using AI (ChatGPT)”

Waxyaabaha Muhiimka ah:

  • Thumbnail qurux badan
  • Description cad
  • Keywords sax ah
  • Price competitive ah
---

Step 5: Sida Loo Gaari Karo $100/Day

Tusaale:

  • $10 gig × 10 orders = $100
  • $20 gig × 5 orders = $100

Bilow qiimo yar, kadib marka reviews hesho kordhi qiimaha.
