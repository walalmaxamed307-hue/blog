Overview & Consent

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At TechSomali, accessible from our platform, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by TechSomali and how we use it.

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. This policy is strictly maintained to meet the latest global standards for online data safety and user security.

Information We Collect

TechSomali does not harvest personal information automatically. If you contact us directly via email, contact forms, or newsletter subscriptions, we may receive explicit details such as your name, email address, or message contents.

Formspree Notice: We utilize Formspree as a secure and encrypted service to handle user submissions and inquiries transmitted through our contact pages.

Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies to store visitor preferences and optimize user experiences. Additionally, this platform integrates Google Analytics to monitor general visitor volume, interactions, and demographic trends.

This data is completely anonymized and processed solely to create better technology, AI, and programming content for our digital community.

Google AdSense & Third-Party Ads

Google is one of the third-party vendors integrated into our site. It utilizes cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve targeted ads to our visitors based on their web history across this site and others on the internet. Visitors can choose to decline the use of DART cookies by accessing the official Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at Google’s Ads Settings.

Log Files Data

TechSomali follows a standard administrative procedure of utilizing log files. These logs are strictly decoupled from any personally identifiable information. The recorded metrics include:

✔ Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
✔ Browser type and application version
✔ Internet Service Provider (ISP)
✔ Date and time stamps
✔ Referring / exit navigation pages
✔ Total number of platform clicks

We ensure you are fully aware of all your legal data protection rights. Every visitor interacting with our services is entitled to the following liberties:

  • The right to object to processing – You retain the right to object to how we process or manage your data parameters.
