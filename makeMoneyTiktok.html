1. Creator Rewards: TikTok Ma Si Toos Ah Ayuu Kuu Lacag Bixiyaa?

TikTok wuxuu leeyahay barnaamijyo rasmi ah oo creators-ka qaarkood loogu oggolaado inay dakhli ka helaan content-kooda, laakiin barnaamijyadan ma aha kuwo dal kasta laga heli karo, shuruudahooduna way is beddeli karaan.

Tusaale ahaan, TikTok wuxuu leeyahay Creator Rewards Program, kaas oo loogu talagalay creators buuxiya shuruudo gaar ah. Shuruudaha, eligibility-ga iyo meelaha laga heli karo waa in si toos ah looga hubiyaa TikTok, sababtoo ah xogta rasmiga ahi way is beddeli kartaa.

Waxaad ka hubin kartaa macluumaadka rasmiga ah ee TikTok Creator Rewards: TikTok Creator Rewards Program .

Haddii aad awooddo inaad TikTok ka hesho lacag toos ah, waa faa'iido. Laakiin haddii barnaamijkaasi aanu dalkaaga ka jirin, weli waxaad isticmaali kartaa TikTok si aad u dhisto audience aad adigu leedahay.

2. TikTok LIVE: Sidee Live Looga Samayn Karaa Lacag?

Dadka daawanaya LIVE-ka waxay ku siin karaan taageero iyadoo ku xiran features-ka TikTok ee dalkaaga iyo account-kaaga. TikTok wuxuu leeyahay nidaamyo kala duwan oo ku saabsan LIVE Gifts iyo features kale, laakiin eligibility-ga iyo availability-ga waa in laga hubiyaa TikTok.

Si kastaba ha ahaatee, LIVE-ku ma aha oo keliya meel lacag lagu raadiyo. Waa meel aad ku dhisi karto trust. Haddii aad tusaale ahaan ka hadasho coding, waxaad samayn kartaa LIVE aad dadka ugu sharaxayso React, JavaScript ama sida website loo dhiso.

Marka dadka ay ku aaminaan, fursadahaaga dakhli waxay ka imaan karaan meel ka baxsan LIVE-ka laftiisa:

3. Affiliate Marketing: Habka Ugu Awoodda Badan ee TikTok Looga Helo Dakhli

Haddii aad rabto inaad TikTok u isticmaasho sidii traffic machine, affiliate marketing waa mid ka mid ah hababka ugu xiisaha badan.

Affiliate marketing micnihiisu waa inaad dadka u gudbiso product ama adeeg ay shirkad kale leedahay. Haddii qof uu sameeyo action u qalma commission-ka, adiga ayaa helaya lacag.

Tusaale ahaan, haddii aad leedahay TikTok account ka hadla AI tools, waxaad samayn kartaa video leh cinwaan:

"5 AI Tools oo Freelancer kasta uu u baahan yahay 2026."

Waxaad sharxaysaa tools-ka, waxaad tusaysaa sida loo isticmaalo, kadibna waxaad dadka u tilmaamaysaa meel ay ka heli karaan macluumaad dheeraad ah.

Haddii program-ka affiliate-ka uu oggol yahay traffic-kaaga iyo habka aad u xayeysiinayso, waxaad heli kartaa commission marka qof sameeyo purchase ama action kale oo u qalma.

Laakiin hal qalad ha samayn: ha xayeysiin product aadan aqoon. Haddii aad rabto inaad muddo dheer dhisto audience, kalsoonidu waa lacagta ugu weyn.

Waxaad sidoo kale ka akhrisan kartaa maqaal kale oo TechSomalia ah oo ku saabsan sida affiliate marketing looga shaqeysto .

Haddii aad rabto inaad fahanto sida affiliate marketing u shaqeeyo, waxaad sidoo kale eegi kartaa FTC's guidance on endorsements and affiliate disclosures .
