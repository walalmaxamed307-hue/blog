Haa. Waxaa jira creator monetization features marka aad eligible tahay, laakiin sidoo kale waxaad TikTok uga samayn kartaa lacag affiliate marketing, sponsorships, adeegyo, digital products iyo website traffic.

Sidee TikTok lacag looga sameeyaa Soomaaliya?

Hubi features-ka hadda Soomaaliya ka shaqeeya, laakiin xitaa haddii feature gaar ah aanu kuu available ahayn, waxaad TikTok u isticmaali kartaa audience, clients, affiliate traffic, website traffic iyo business leads.

Immisa followers ayaa lacag lagu bilaabi karaa?

Waxay ku xiran tahay habka monetization-ka. Creator programs waxay leeyihiin shuruudo gaar ah, halka adeegyo, affiliate iyo sponsorships ay ku xirnaan karaan niche iyo audience.
