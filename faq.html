📌 General

Waa maxay TechSomalia?

TechSomalia waa platform tech education ah oo lagu barto AI, coding, SEO, blogging iyo online income. Ujeeddadiisu waa in dhalinyarada Soomaaliyeed lagu baro xirfado casri ah oo ay internet-ka uga shaqeystaan ama uga sameeyaan dakhli. Wax walba waxaa lagu sharxaa si fudud oo beginner-friendly ah.

Yaa iska leh TechSomalia?

TechSomalia waxaa sameeyay hal-abuure Somali ah oo diiradda saaraya digital education. Ujeeddadu waa in la dhiso platform aqoon tech ah oo dadka Soomaaliyeed ka caawiya inay la jaanqaadaan dunida digital-ka ah.

TechSomalia maxaa loogu talagalay?

Waxaa loogu talagalay in dadka lagu baro xirfado casri ah sida coding, AI tools, freelancing, SEO iyo digital marketing si ay internet-ka uga shaqeeyaan ama dakhli uga sameeyaan.

💡 Skills & Learning

Xirfadaha ugu fiican 2026 waa maxay?

2026 xirfadaha ugu muhiimsan waa Web Development, AI Tools & Prompt Engineering, Digital Marketing, SEO, Graphic Design, Video Editing iyo Freelancing. Kuwani waa skills si weyn looga baahan yahay suuqa shaqada online-ka.

Ma bilaabi karaa haddii aan eber ahay?

Haa, gabi ahaanba waad bilaabi kartaa xitaa haddii aadan wax khibrad ah lahayn. Casharrada waxaa loo sameeyay beginners si wax walba si fudud loo fahmo, kadibna si tartiib ah loo gaaro heer xirfadeed.

Coding ma adagtahay?

Coding ma aha wax adag haddii si sax ah loo barto. Haddii aad ka bilowdo HTML, CSS iyo JavaScript, waxaad si tartiib ah u fahmi doontaa sida websites u shaqeeyaan. Practice joogto ah ayaa muhiim ah.

💰 Money & Online Income

Sidee lacag looga sameeyaa internet-ka?

Lacag internet-ka waxaa looga sameeyaa blogging, freelancing, affiliate marketing, YouTube iyo AI content creation. Waxa ugu muhiimsan waa inaad barato skill suuq leh kadibna aad si joogto ah u isticmaasho.

SEO maxay muhiim u tahay?

SEO waa habka lagu hagaajiyo website si uu uga soo muuqdo Google. Waxay keentaa traffic bilaash ah oo organic ah, taasoo ka dhigta website-kaaga mid booqasho badan hela.

Freelancing waa maxay?

Freelancing waa shaqo online ah oo aad ku bixiso adeegyo sida design, writing, coding ama marketing adigoon shirkad joogto ah u shaqeyn.

Blogging sidee lacag looga sameeyaa?

Blogging waxaa lacag looga sameeyaa Google Ads, affiliate marketing iyo sponsorships. Marka traffic-kaagu bato, dakhliga sidoo kale wuu kordhaa.

AI tools waxaa loo isticmaalaa content writing, design, coding assistance, automation iyo video editing. Waxay kaa caawiyaan inaad shaqooyin degdeg u qabato.
