Baro Isticmaalka ChatGPT iyo AI Kale Si Sax Ah – Hagaha Qofka Bilowga ah

By TechSomali • Febraayo 2026 • 5 min read

Bal ka fikir inaad haysato caawiye maskax badan leh oo aan daalin, oo 24/7 diyaar kuu ah, kaa caawinaya qoraal, design, research, coding iyo xitaa lacag sameyn. Taasi waa ChatGPT iyo AI tools-ka casriga ah.

Laakiin xaqiiqdu waxay tahay: 90% dadka isticmaala AI si qaldan ayay u isticmaalaan. Waxay weydiiyaan su’aalo gaaban, natiijo liita ayay helaan, kadibna waxay yiraahdaan “AI waxba ma taro.”

Maqaalkan waxaad ku baran doontaa sida saxda ah ee loo isticmaalo ChatGPT iyo AI kale si aad uga faa’iidaysato waxbarasho, shaqo, freelancing iyo ganacsi online ah.

Waa Maxay ChatGPT iyo AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) waa tiknoolajiyad u oggolaanaysa computer-ka inuu qabto hawlo u baahan fikir sida bani’aadamka.

ChatGPT waa AI chatbot awood u leh:

  • Qoraal samayn
  • Su’aalo ka jawaabid
  • Barnaamijyo sharaxid
  • Content creation
  • Idea generation

Laakiin ChatGPT ma aha sixir. Tayada natiijada waxay ku xiran tahay sida aad u isticmaasho.

Maxay Dadku Si Qaldan U Isticmaalaan AI?

Khaladaadka ugu badan:

  • Prompt gaaban oo aan faahfaahin lahayn
  • Inay AI ka filaan shaqo dhan hal amar
  • Inay copy/paste sameeyaan adigoon fahmin
  • Inay AI u arkaan “Google beddelkiisa” kaliya

AI ma aha search engine kaliya, waa tool aad u baahan tahay inaad hagto.

Sidee Loo Qoraa Prompt Sax Ah?

Prompt waa amarka aad siiso AI. Haddii prompt-kaagu liito, natiijada waa liidataa.

Prompt Liita

write article about AI

Prompt Xooggan

Write a detailed 2000-word article about AI for beginners in Somali language, include headings, examples, SEO keywords, and practical use cases for freelancing and online income.

Farqiga natiijada waa aad u weyn.

Qaabka PRO Prompt (Formula)

Isticmaal qaabkan:

  • Role (cidda AI noqonayso)
  • Task (waxa aad rabto)
  • Context (macluumaad)
  • Format (qaabka natiijada)
  • Constraints (xaddidaad)

Tusaale:

You are a professional SEO copywriter. Write a 1500-word blog post about AI tools in Somali. Use headings, examples, and make it beginner friendly.
