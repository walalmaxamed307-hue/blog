Frequently Asked Questions

Su’aalaha ugu muhiimsan ee ku saabsan TechSomalia, barashada tech, iyo lacag samaynta online.

Waa maxay TechSomalia?

TechSomalia waa website loogu talagalay barashada technology-ga, AI tools, coding, digital skills iyo online income. Ujeeddadu waa in dhalinyarada Soomaaliyeed lagu caawiyo xirfado casri ah oo ay shaqo ama dakhli kaga sameyn karaan internet-ka.

Yaa iska leh TechSomalia?

TechSomalia waxaa sameeyay hal-abuure Somali ah oo diiradda saaraya tech education iyo digital growth. Website-ku wuxuu ku dhisan yahay fikir ah in aqoonta tech-ga laga dhigo mid fudud oo qof walba fahmi karo.

Sidee lacag looga sameeyaa TechSomalia ama internet-ka?

Lacag sameynta internet-ka waxay ku xirantahay xirfaddaada. Waxaad isticmaali kartaa: blogging + ads, affiliate marketing, freelancing ama AI tools si aad u sameyso content ama adeegyo. TechSomalia waxay ku baraysaa sida xirfadahaas loo barto loona isticmaalo.

Ma bilaabi karaa xitaa haddii aan eber ahay?

Haa. TechSomalia waxaa loogu talagalay bilowga (beginners). Wax walba waxaa lagu sharxaa si fudud, talaabo-talaabo ah si aad uga bilowdo eber ilaa heer xirfad leh.
